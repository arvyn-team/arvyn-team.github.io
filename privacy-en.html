Privacy policy

This notice explains what data I collect through ARVYN, why I collect it, who I share it with and what you can do to control it, as required by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR). This is a translation: in case of discrepancies, the Italian version prevails.

1. Data controller

Matteo Mucciolo — contact: arvynapp@gmail.com.

2. What data I collect

Account data: email, name, password (never stored in plain text, only in encrypted form).

Onboarding data: sex, age, height, weight, occupational activity level, daily steps, goal (lose weight/maintain/build muscle), target weight, training experience, any notes on injuries or physical limitations that you provide yourself.

Usage data: logged meals (with photos, text descriptions or chat messages, and the calculated nutritional values), logged workouts (exercises, sets, reps, weights, duration, calories estimated with a standard formula, not by artificial intelligence), conversations with the AI coach, Body roadmap photos and weight, coach memory (the information you choose to have it remember, for example goals, injuries or preferences), multi-week training programs, meal plans and shopping list (with the preferences you choose: style, foods to exclude such as lactose-containing dairy or gluten, time available), recipes saved or created with the coach, logged fasts (start, end and chosen schedule, for example 16:8), daily step goal, cardio activities (type, km, time, incline, perceived exertion, time for each km), weekly cardio goal, shoes and their km, orders placed in the shop, credit balance and usage.

Data from your phone's health app (only if you connect Apple Health or Health Connect from Settings › Health app): steps, hours of sleep, last recorded weight and cardio workouts (type, duration and distance), read-only. This data is read on the phone when needed to show it to you and is not stored on my servers, with two exceptions. If you tap “Import” on the Cardio page, the workouts you choose become workouts logged in ARVYN (type, date, duration and distance), as if you had entered them yourself. If you turn on “Count steps in calories burned”, ARVYN receives and stores your total steps for each day (the last 8 days each time you open the app) in order to add to your calories burned the steps above your activity level; turning it off, or deleting your account, erases them. ARVYN does not write anything to your health app, does not use this data for advertising and does not pass it on to others. If you turn on “Use in AI coach”, each time you ask the coach a question only your average steps and average hours of sleep over the last 7 days are sent to Anthropic, without being stored. You can disconnect and revoke permissions at any time.

Data that stays only on your phone: during live cardio, the steps read from the phone's pedometer and your step length stay on the phone; ARVYN only receives the km, the time and the time for each km of the session you save. Photos of meals you choose to log later stay in the app's storage on your phone: they are not uploaded until you tap “Estimate” and are deleted when you estimate or delete them, and in any case after 3 days. Reminders (end of fast, pending photos) are notifications created by the phone itself, without going through my servers. Widgets and Live Activities show the day's calories, macros and workout from a copy of this data stored on the phone. Images to share (end of workout and Body roadmap) are created on the phone: the photo you take or choose for the image is not uploaded to my servers; for the Body roadmap “Before and after”, the photos from your check-ins are downloaded to the phone only to compose the image. The image leaves your phone only if you tap “Share” and choose where to send it (for example Instagram or WhatsApp): from that moment, the privacy policy of the app you chose applies.

Payment data (Shop only): when you buy a product, your card details are handled directly by Stripe on a secure payment page hosted by Stripe itself — ARVYN never receives or stores your full card number.

Automatic technical data: if the app runs into an error, a technical report (without the content of your meals or messages) is automatically sent to the Sentry monitoring service to help me fix the problem.

3. Why I collect it and on what legal basis

Account, onboarding, usage and payment data: I process it to provide you with the service you requested — calculating your goals, estimating calories, giving advice through the AI coach, managing orders and credits (legal basis: performance of the contract with you, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). For data concerning health, see also section 4 below.

Technical error data (Sentry): I process it on the basis of my legitimate interest in keeping the app working and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

I do not use your data for advertising and I do not sell it to third parties.

4. Health data — special category of data

Weight, goals, logged meals and workouts are considered data concerning health under Art. 9 GDPR, a specially protected category of data. I process them only with your explicit consent, which you give by ticking the box during sign-up — you can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account (section 7).

The coach memory may contain health information (for example an injury): the coach may offer to remember something you wrote to it, but it is saved only if you tap “Remember” or if you write it yourself, and you can edit or delete it at any time from “What ARVYN knows”.

The foods you exclude from your meal plan (for example lactose-containing dairy or gluten) and the fasts you log may also concern your health: I process them with the same safeguards.

5. Who I share data with

Some data is passed on to technical providers that help me run ARVYN, only to the extent strictly necessary to provide the service:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — receives meal photos, the messages you write in the chat about your meals and your questions to the coach, together with the latest messages of the conversation (at most 6, from the last 7 days), the coach memory and some profile data (weight, goal, experience, workouts of the week), in order to generate nutritional estimates and coach replies. For AI workout plans and multi-week programs it receives your goal, equipment, experience, the injury note and the coach memory. For the custom recipe it receives the ingredients you write, the meal, the time available, the foods to exclude, the calorie and protein target for that meal and the coach memory (without the injury notes). For the coach check-ins (weekly, mid-month workouts and monthly) it receives only a summary of the period (average calories and protein, number and minutes of workouts, training volume, the names of the most-trained exercises and of personal records, days on which the water goal was reached, weight trend) and the goal you chose, plus the coach memory if you have filled it in, without your name, email or photos. It does not receive the complete list of your workouts (calories burned are calculated with a standard formula, without artificial intelligence).
  • Stripe — receives payment data when you buy in the Shop, handled entirely on its secure platform.
  • RevenueCat — manages the subscriptions and top-ups you buy in the App Store or on Google Play: it receives your ARVYN account's internal ID (not your name or email) and the purchase data sent by the store (product, date, expiry, country). It does not receive your card details: Apple or Google handle the payment.
  • Resend — receives your email address in order to send you service emails (account verification, password reset).
  • Railway — hosts the database where all your data is stored.
  • Sentry — receives automatic technical reports if the app runs into an error, without the content of your meals or messages.
  • Open Food Facts — a free public database of packaged products. When you scan a barcode, our server sends it only the code number (printed on the product), without any data that identifies you; the camera image is neither saved nor sent to anyone. When you search for a packaged product by name, our server sends it only the words you typed, again without any data that identifies you. The search for basic foods takes place on our server, without going through third parties.

None of these providers is authorized to use your data for its own purposes.

6. Transfer of data outside the European Union

Anthropic, Stripe, RevenueCat, Resend and Sentry are companies based in the United States: the data they receive may therefore also be processed outside the European Economic Area. These providers adhere to standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission or to equivalent mechanisms to ensure an adequate level of protection for your data. [To be verified and confirmed with each provider, with legal support if needed, before public release — the hosting region chosen on Railway must also be confirmed.]

7. How long I keep data

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account (Settings → Delete account), all your data — profile, workouts and programs, meals, meal plans and recipes, fasts, orders, messages and coach memory — is permanently deleted from the live database. It nevertheless remains, for up to 30 days, in the automatic backup copies of the database (used only in the event of serious failures, never consulted for any other purpose), after which it is deleted as well.

8. Data security

Your password is stored only in encrypted form (never in plain text), connections between the app and the server are encrypted, and access to data is protected by authentication. No system is 100% invulnerable, but I take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

9. Automated decision-making and artificial intelligence

Nutritional estimates, coach suggestions and AI responses are generated automatically and are neither a diagnosis nor medical advice — see the app's medical disclaimer for details. I do not make decisions that produce legal effects or similarly significant effects on you based solely on automated processing.

10. Minors

ARVYN is intended for use by adults. I do not knowingly collect data from persons under 18; if you believe a minor has created an account, write to me at arvynapp@gmail.com to have it deleted.

11. Your rights

At any time you can:

  • Download a complete copy of your data (Settings → Export my data).
  • Correct your data (Settings → edit profile).
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated data (Settings → Delete account).
  • Withdraw the consent given during sign-up (for health data, this has the same effect as deleting your account).

For any other request concerning your data, you can write to arvynapp@gmail.com.

You also always have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Roma — garante@gpdp.it — www.garanteprivacy.it), if you believe that the processing of your data infringes the law.

12. Changes to this policy

If I substantially change how I use your data, I will update this page and let you know in the app.
